Concepts

Resin halo

Graded stiffness from opened foam cells along saw-cuts — not a sharp kerf only.

Grid-scoring (knife or saw cuts) does more than leave a neat resin-filled kerf: the cut opens foam cells along groove walls and root. Those opened cells take up resin during infusion, so the real cross-section is graded:

Laustsen et al. (2014) CT scans show this interface is wider than the nominal slit and depends on foam density — smaller cells (e.g. H130) give a thinner resin-rich band than larger cells (e.g. H60). b3_core models this explicitly instead of absorbing it into a calibrated failure strain alone.

How stiffness is graded

For each point in the foam (outside the neat kerf), compute the distance d [mm] to the nearest cut surface — groove side walls and groove root only. The unsawn outer top/bottom faces do not emit a full saw-cut halo (there is no opened-cell cut there).

Two surface types carry independent halos; the field takes the maximum:

SurfaceWhereCell stateDefault reach
saw_cutGroove walls + rootOpened by saw/knifefrom core.cell_size
faceUnsawn z=0 / z=thicknessClosed cells0.25 × saw-cut reach

Each S(d) is the survival function of that surface's cell-size distribution: S(0)=1 at the cut face, S→0 at reach.

Local stiffness at each Gauss point in foam cells is a rule of mixtures:

Bigger cells ⇒ longer reach ⇒ wider resin-rich band ⇒ higher homogenized moduli (tests confirm Ezz increases monotonically with cell_size).

Backend and outputs

Halo needs per-Gauss-point stiffness, so cases with core.cell_size set auto-route to the numpy backend (ccx/mfem are two-phase isotropic integrators). Results include:

  • resin_vf — neat kerf resin volume fraction
  • halo_vf — extra resin from opened cells in the foam band
  • effective_resin_vf = resin_vf + halo_vf
  • rho_infused — density including halo resin

For FEA handoff, the homogenized engineering constants already embed the graded stiffness — no separate halo layer is needed in the global model.

When mould curvature is also set, the halo grades off the tapered wall (hw(z)), not the flat rectangular tag — see Halo + curvature. Stiffness response vs both kx and cell_size: parametric boards.
