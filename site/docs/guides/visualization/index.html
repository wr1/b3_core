Visualization & reports

Gallery boards, datasheets, deformation montages, and resin-halo figures.

Figures and reports are optional — they are not required for FEA handoff. The b3_core.viz layer shares one model cache (CoreModel), one theme (CoreTheme), and a fluent scene builder (CoreScene).
